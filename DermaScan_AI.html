How it works

The app sends each photo to an image model trained in Google Teachable Machine. The model compares it with the examples it learned from and gives a confidence for each class.

Who might it fail?

Most public skin photo collections have fewer photos of darker skin. A model trained on them may be less accurate for some people. It was also trained on close-up specialist photos, so ordinary phone photos may confuse it.

Privacy

The model runs inside your browser. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.
